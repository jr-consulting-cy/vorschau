Greg’s on Tombs of the Kings Avenue

Greg’s His & Hers Hair Salon is at Tombs of the Kings Avenue 7 in Paphos, behind a glass front with a navy sign that says All about hair. Inside: pink walls, tall mirrors, black styling chairs, two brown wash chairs and a red chair with a horse head for children. Appointments by phone or WhatsApp, as the salon writes on Instagram.
