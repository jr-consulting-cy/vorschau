Two shops, one team

Fade Barber Shop has two shops in the centre of Limassol, on Gladstonos 66 and on Ellados 83, with one phone number for both. The profile speaks of more than ten years of experience, skin fades, kids cuts and outcalls. Regulars come back, and visitors write too: one came off a cruise ship for a single day in Limassol and got a haircut there.
