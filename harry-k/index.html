Barbers by family

Barber Shop by Harry K is run by two brothers who call themselves third-generation barbers. According to one review, the family works in the trade in Cardiff, Wales. Today the shop is on Filiou Zannetou, a few steps from Finikoudes, with green walls and classic barber chairs.
