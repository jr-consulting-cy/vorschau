A pet shop on Zakynthou

PET BAZAAR sits in a white building on Zakynthou 24, with the red and yellow sign that says what the shop is about: all about pets for less. Customers describe a simple, warehouse-style shop that stocks pretty much everything, where some customers have been buying their pet food for years.
