All day, in the heart of the Marina

At Azura, simple and familiar food becomes deeper through skill, respect for ingredients and natural cooking techniques. The kitchen works with the fundamental elements, land, water and fire, to bring out true flavours.

A place where it feels natural to be yourself, morning to evening: breakfast at the weekend, lunch, an evening by the water. Dogs are welcome.
