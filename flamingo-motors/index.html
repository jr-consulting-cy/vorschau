About us

Four decades on Omonia Avenue

Flamingo Motor Agency Ltd has been satisfying motoring needs for over four decades. Established in the 1980s, the dealership has grown continuously and is today one of the most reliable new and used car dealers in Cyprus, and one of its largest independent ones.
