What the van does

From the Google listing, the Facebook page and what drivers write in their reviews.

Puncture repair on the spot

Nails and screws taken out and the tyre repaired where the car stands, often in ten to fifteen minutes.

Tyre change

If the tyre cannot be saved, the van changes it on site. One driver writes that a rare size was found on a Sunday.

Tyres for every car

Tyre sales and repairs, all types of tyres, as the Facebook page puts it.

A workshop on wheels

Nikos Patsias Mobile Tyre Services is a white van with a tyre machine inside, on the road in and around Limassol. You call, and the van comes to where the car stands, in town or on the side of the road. On the Facebook page the service also lists Larnaca, Nicosia, Paphos and Famagusta.
