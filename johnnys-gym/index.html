A new gym in Paphos

Johnny’s Gym opened about a year ago in a large glass building on the B7 in Paphos, with the name in big letters on the roof. Inside there are two floors: strength machines, racks and free weights downstairs, cardio on the gallery above, all on dark rubber floors under white ceilings. On Instagram the gym calls itself your second home.
