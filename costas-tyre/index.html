Services

What the workshop does

From the workshop’s own website and its Google reviews.

Tyres

Over 5000 tyres in stock, from budget to high performance and run-flat, for cars, 4x4 and trucks, motorcycles and ATVs, agricultural and industrial vehicles.

Punctures and repairs

Customers write about punctures fixed in 5 to 20 minutes, and that the other tyres were checked at the same time. One review says they will also come to you if you cannot drive to them.

Wheel alignment and rims

Wheel alignment to the manufacturer’s specifications, and bent steel or alloy rims straightened for a fraction of the cost of a new wheel, as the website says.

Batteries

A wide range of batteries for many makes and models, with a two-year guarantee, as the website says.

How customers describe a visit

Taken from what customers write in their Google reviews.

Fast

A puncture fixed in 15 minutes on a Saturday morning, a set of four tyres fitted in about 40 minutes, as customers write.

Clean

Six reviews praise how clean the workshop is, one calls it well-organized and spotless.

Coffee while you wait

Customers mention coffee or a cold drink, a couch with Wi-Fi at reception and a relaxed waiting corner.

A big tyre workshop in Mesogi

Costas Tyre Service works on Mesogi Avenue in Mesogi, Paphos, in a bright hall with several bays for fitting, balancing and alignment. One customer thinks it is the biggest tyre service in Paphos, and several write that they have come back for years.
