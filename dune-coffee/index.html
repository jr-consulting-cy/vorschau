A coffee shop on Ellados

Dune Coffee is a small specialty coffee shop at Ellados 80 in Limassol. Guests write about wooden furniture, nice glassware, a quiet courtyard and a menu that keeps getting better. On Instagram the cafe calls itself specialty coffee in the heart of Limassol.
