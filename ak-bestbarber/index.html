A barbershop on Griva Digeni

AK BESTBARBER is at Georgiou Griva Digeni 136 in Limassol, with a black sign, winged scissors and a barber pole outside. Photos inside show a sofa, a guitar, a hat and antlers on wooden walls. On Instagram the shop writes that it now also has Russian and Ukrainian speakers.
