Straight from the grill

From the menu and from what guests write in their Google reviews.

Meze

Halloumi, ribs, sausage, souvlaki, sheftalia and liver, with pita, salad and fries. For one, two or four people.

Souvlaki and sheftalia

Pork or chicken, in a Cypriot pita or as a portion with potatoes. Guests praise the sheftalia in particular.

Without meat

Grilled vegetables as a portion, or halloumi, mushrooms or falafel in pita.

Eat in or take away

Sit down at the tavern, pick up your order or have it delivered.

On the grill, in two languages

Epi Sxaras means “on the grill” in Greek, and In Craticula says the same in Latin. A neighbourhood tavern in a quiet residential street of Nicosia, Cypriot and Greek dishes from the grill.
