A private studio on 28 October Avenue

DotismLoom is a private tattoo studio on 28 October Avenue in Limassol, by appointment only. The walls are covered with framed drawings, the window holds cacti, antlers and a brass bell. Reviews go back about nine years, and many clients write that they keep coming back for their next tattoo.
