Your neighbourhood coffee spot

Nôtre Café is on Danaes Avenue in Paphos and calls itself a neighbourhood coffee spot on Instagram. Inside: pale mint walls, a crystal chandelier, wooden tables and chairs, pink armchairs and shelves full of books. Guests describe it as light and bright with a touch of grandma kitsch.
