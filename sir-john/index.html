A barbershop in Emba

Sir John Barber Shop is at Nikolaou Ellina 99A in Emba, under a black and white striped awning and a round sign that says est. 2018. Inside are a chrome barber chair, a petrol blue wall and leather sofas. Many clients are locals, many are visitors who drop in for a holiday cut.
